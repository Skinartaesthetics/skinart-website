What Is BioRePeel?

BioRePeel is a two-phase, biostimulating chemical peel made with 35% TCA (trichloroacetic acid) combined with a blend of amino acids, vitamins (B2, C), gamma-aminobutyric acid (GABA), and other actives. The formula works on two levels simultaneously: the lipophilic (oil-soluble) phase exfoliates the upper skin layers, while the hydrophilic (water-soluble) phase delivers nutrients and stimulates fibroblast activity deeper in the skin.

The result is controlled exfoliation, collagen and elastin stimulation, and visible improvement in texture, tone, and clarity — all without the visible shedding, prolonged recovery, or sun sensitivity that come with traditional TCA peels at comparable strengths.

What BioRePeel Can Address

BioRePeel is effective for a wide range of skin concerns. Clients most commonly come to SkinArt for BioRePeel to improve skin texture and pore appearance, address mild to moderate hyperpigmentation and post-breakout discoloration, reduce dullness and improve radiance, manage acne-prone skin and congestion, and support early anti-aging goals by stimulating collagen production.

Because it carries minimal photosensitivity risk, it is particularly popular as a year-round treatment alternative when summer heat and sun exposure make traditional peels less practical.

Who Is a Good Candidate for BioRePeel?

BioRePeel is suitable for most adult skin types, including combination, oily, dry, and sensitive skin. It is well-tolerated by a range of skin tones. It is one of the few professional exfoliation treatments that can be performed safely in summer without special precautions beyond regular SPF use.

BioRePeel may not be appropriate if you are pregnant or nursing, have active rashes, open wounds, or severely compromised skin, or are in an active flare of a skin condition. We review your skin and health history at every consultation before proceeding.

What to Expect During Your BioRePeel Session

Your BioRePeel appointment at SkinArt Aesthetics takes approximately 60 minutes from start to finish. The treatment begins with a thorough cleanse and skin prep. The BioRePeel solution is then applied in a specific layered technique, left to work for the appropriate time, and then massaged gently into the skin — there is no neutralization required, which is one of the distinctive aspects of the formula.

During the treatment, you may experience mild tingling or a light warming sensation, which typically fades within minutes. After the session, your skin will be bright and slightly flushed for a few hours. Most clients return to normal activities the same day.

Recovery and Aftercare

One of the most valued aspects of BioRePeel is its minimal downtime. Most clients experience no visible peeling, though some may notice very light, barely visible flaking in areas of heavier congestion. This typically resolves within 48–72 hours.

After your BioRePeel:

  • Wear SPF 30+ daily — every day, not just when you plan to be outdoors
  • Keep your routine simple for 24–48 hours: gentle cleanser, light moisturizer, sunscreen
  • Avoid heavy exfoliants, retinoids, and acid products for at least 5 days
  • Avoid sweating heavily, saunas, and steam rooms for 24 hours
  • Do not pick or rub at the skin if any light flaking occurs

Full aftercare guidelines are available on our Post Care page.

How Often Should You Get BioRePeel?

For ongoing skin health and maintenance, BioRePeel can be performed monthly or as part of a quarterly treatment plan. For specific goals like correcting discoloration or improving texture, a series of 4–6 peels spaced 2–3 weeks apart is often recommended. We create a personalized schedule at your consultation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does BioRePeel actually peel your skin?

Despite the name, BioRePeel typically produces little to no visible shedding. The exfoliation happens at the cellular level, and the skin renews itself without the visible peeling associated with traditional peels. A small percentage of clients with heavy congestion may notice very light, subtle flaking after 48–72 hours.

Can I get BioRePeel in summer?

Yes — this is one of BioRePeel’s key advantages. The formula carries minimal photosensitivity risk, making it safe to receive year-round, including during summer months. Daily SPF is still required, as it is after any professional treatment.

How many BioRePeel treatments will I need?

A single session produces visible improvement, and many clients choose to integrate BioRePeel into their regular maintenance routine. For targeted correction of texture or pigmentation, a series of 4–6 sessions is generally recommended. We discuss your goals and recommend a plan at your consultation.

Is BioRePeel safe for sensitive skin?

BioRePeel is generally well-tolerated by sensitive skin due to its balanced formula and the absence of visible irritation. That said, each client’s sensitivity is different, and we always assess your skin’s current state before proceeding. If your skin is compromised, actively reactive, or in a flare, we may recommend a gentler starting point.

How does BioRePeel compare to a traditional chemical peel?

Traditional chemical peels rely primarily on acid exfoliation and vary in depth from very superficial to deep. BioRePeel combines TCA exfoliation with a biostimulating complex that also nourishes and stimulates the skin, without the visible peeling or extended recovery of a comparable-strength traditional peel. The trade-off is a gentler visible effect per session; the benefit is versatility, year-round use, and virtually no downtime.
