**Always include your location** — at minimum "Huntingdon Valley, PA" — in every post body. GBP posts are local signals, and Google uses the text to associate your profile with local searches.

**Use natural service mentions**, not keyword strings: "customized facial in Huntingdon Valley" reads better than "facial Huntingdon Valley PA."

**Photos make posts perform better.** Use your existing professional photos (DSC series, menu photos) and vary them week to week. Avoid stock photos — Google favors authentic business photos.
