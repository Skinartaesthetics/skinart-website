Q-switched Nd:YAG laser removal for body tattoos, microblading, powder brows, lip liner, eyeliner, and other permanent makeup.

Whether you’re looking to fully remove a tattoo, fade permanent makeup that no longer reflects your style, or lighten an existing brow tattoo before a correction, SkinArt Aesthetics offers professional Q-switched Nd:YAG laser removal in a clinical, boutique setting. Powder brows and ombre brows are among the most common permanent makeup removal requests. The pigments used in powder brow procedures can vary widely by artist and brand, and some pigments — particularly those containing iron oxides — may paradoxically darken when first exposed to laser energy before they begin to fade. We proceed carefully and conservatively with all PMU removal, especially on the face.

How Q-Switched Nd:YAG Laser Removal Works

The Q-switched Nd:YAG laser delivers extremely short pulses of laser energy at specific wavelengths that are absorbed by tattoo and PMU pigment. This energy shatters the ink or pigment particles into smaller fragments. The body’s immune system then identifies and gradually eliminates these fragments through the lymphatic system over the weeks following each session — which is why results are progressive rather than immediate.

The laser operates at two primary wavelengths: 1064 nm, which targets darker pigments including black, dark blue, and dark brown, and 532 nm, which is more effective for lighter colors including red, orange, and some greens. No single wavelength removes all ink colors equally, which is why a full assessment of the tattoo’s color composition informs the treatment approach.

PMU pigments present a specific challenge because they often contain iron oxides, which can paradoxically darken on initial laser exposure before fading. This is a known and manageable reaction, but it requires conservative settings and careful sequencing, particularly on the face and sensitive areas.

What Factors Affect How Many Sessions I’ll Need?

Tattoo and PMU removal is not a single-session process. The number of sessions required depends on several factors:

  • Ink or pigment color: Black and dark inks respond most readily to laser treatment. Bright colors — particularly yellow, white, and light green — are more resistant
  • Ink depth and density: Professional tattoos tend to be placed deeper and more densely than amateur work or PMU, requiring more sessions
  • Age of the tattoo or PMU: Older tattoos have typically already begun fading, which can accelerate removal
  • Skin tone: Lighter skin allows more aggressive settings with less risk of pigment changes in the surrounding skin
  • Your immune response: Ink clearance happens through the lymphatic system, so your body’s immune activity between sessions directly affects how quickly you see fading
  • Location on the body: Areas with better circulation — like the chest and upper arm — typically respond faster than extremities

Body tattoo removal typically requires 6–12 or more sessions. PMU removal often takes fewer sessions due to shallower pigment placement, but this varies. We will give you an honest assessment at your consultation based on what we see — not a number designed to set a low expectation.

Preparing for Your Removal Session

  • Avoid sun exposure and tanning (including self-tanner) on the treatment area for at least 4 weeks prior
  • Keep the skin in the treatment area moisturized and healthy in the weeks leading up to your appointment
  • Avoid retinoids and chemical exfoliants on the area for at least 7 days before treatment
  • Arrive with clean skin — no lotion, makeup, or fragrance on the treatment area
  • Inform us of any medications you are taking, particularly those that affect photosensitivity or immune response

Aftercare for Tattoo & PMU Removal

Proper aftercare after each removal session is essential for both safety and results. In the immediate 24–48 hours after treatment:

  • Keep the treated area clean and dry
  • Apply the recommended soothing ointment or healing balm as instructed
  • Do not pick or rub blistering or crusting that may develop — this is a normal part of the healing response
  • Avoid sun exposure to the treated area — apply SPF 30+ once the skin has healed
  • Do not apply makeup or skincare products to the area until fully healed
  • Allow the full recommended interval between sessions — rushing sessions does not speed overall results

Frequently Asked Questions

Does laser tattoo removal hurt?

Most clients describe the sensation as a series of sharp snaps against the skin, similar to a rubber band. The level of discomfort varies by area and individual pain tolerance. A topical numbing cream can be applied before the session on request. Treatment sessions are brief, which helps with tolerability.

Will my tattoo be completely gone?

Full removal is the goal for most clients, but the degree of clearance depends on factors including ink color, depth, skin tone, and number of sessions completed. Black and dark blue inks respond best; bright colors require more sessions and may not achieve 100% clearance. We will give you a realistic expectation at your consultation based on what we observe.

Can I get my brows tattooed again after removal?

Yes — fading or partial removal is often performed specifically to prepare the skin for a brow correction or re-pigmentation. We can work with your brow artist to determine the level of fading required before the correction. Full removal before re-pigmentation is not always necessary — sometimes significant lightening is all that is needed.

Why did my lip liner get darker after the first session?

Iron-oxide-based pigments used in many PMU procedures can paradoxically darken when first exposed to laser energy. This is a temporary reaction and the pigment will begin to fade in subsequent sessions. This is a known reaction and not a sign that the treatment is going wrong — but it is why we use conservative settings on the first session and why a patch test is recommended before treating the full area.

How long do I need to wait between removal sessions?

A minimum of 6–8 weeks between sessions is recommended for body tattoos to allow the immune system to clear fragmented ink particles and the skin to fully heal. For PMU removal on sensitive facial areas, longer intervals may be advised. Rushing sessions does not speed overall results — the clearing process happens in the weeks between treatments, not during them.
