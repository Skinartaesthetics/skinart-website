What to Expect

Each session takes 15–45 minutes depending on the size and complexity of the area being treated. The laser delivers rapid pulses of energy that shatter ink pigment into smaller particles — your immune system then clears them over the following weeks.

Most clients notice gradual fading after each session. Full removal typically takes 4–12 sessions spaced 6–8 weeks apart for tattoos, and 2–6 sessions for PMU. Darker, older, or amateur tattoos often fade fastest; multi-colored professional tattoos may require more sessions.

Some discomfort is expected — often described as a rubber band snap against the skin. Topical numbing is available on request. Minor redness and swelling resolve within a few days.
