Laser & Body Services

Laser Hair Removal

From $65Varies by area

Permanent hair reduction using professional laser technology targeting the hair follicle while leaving surrounding skin undisturbed. Safe for face, underarms, legs, bikini, back, and other body areas. Multiple sessions are required for permanent results, as the laser is effective only during the active growth phase of each follicle.

Best for: Unwanted facial or body hair, ingrown hairs, skin irritation from shaving or waxing

BBL Photofacial Skin Rejuvenation

Pricing at consultation

BroadBand Light (BBL) is an intense pulsed light treatment that targets sun damage, age spots, redness, and uneven skin tone. The light energy is absorbed by melanin and hemoglobin, breaking down pigmentation and reducing visible vessels at the surface. Results are progressive — skin typically looks brighter and more even after a series of treatments.

Best for: Sun spots, age spots, rosacea flush, diffuse redness, uneven skin tone

Acne BBL

Pricing at consultation

A specialized BBL protocol targeting the bacteria responsible for active acne breakouts as well as reducing post-inflammatory redness left behind by healing lesions. Particularly effective for clients who have not achieved full control through topical treatments alone and want a non-oral option to complement their skincare routine.

Best for: Active acne, inflammatory breakouts, post-acne redness

Carbon Laser Peel

Pricing at consultation

A two-phase treatment in which a carbon solution is applied to the skin and then targeted with laser energy. The carbon absorbs oil, debris, and dead skin cells; the laser then vaporizes the carbon layer, delivering gentle exfoliation, pore tightening, and an overall brightening effect with no downtime. Sometimes called the “China Doll” or “Hollywood” peel.

Best for: Enlarged pores, oily skin, dull complexion, minor texture irregularities, pre-event skin prep

Pigmentation & Vascular Treatments

Pricing at consultation

Targeted laser treatments for discrete pigmented lesions, broken capillaries, and visible vascular irregularities. These treatments address specific spots and vessels rather than treating the full face — making them precise and efficient for concerns that respond well to targeted energy rather than broad-area light therapy.

Best for: Cherry angiomas, spider veins, isolated brown spots, discrete hyperpigmentation

Skin Rejuvenation

Pricing at consultation

Laser-based skin rejuvenation stimulates collagen production and improves the overall texture, tone, and firmness of the skin over a series of treatments. An effective option for clients approaching anti-aging from a laser modality rather than, or in addition to, microneedling or chemical resurfacing.

Best for: Skin laxity, fine lines, dull or uneven texture, overall skin quality

What to Expect at Your Laser Consultation

All laser and body treatments at SkinArt Aesthetics begin with a consultation. This gives us the opportunity to assess your skin tone, hair color (where relevant), current skin health, and any contraindications before recommending a specific protocol. Laser treatments are not universally appropriate for all skin types and conditions, and the consultation ensures the treatment we recommend is the right one for you.

During the consultation we will:

  • Review your current skincare routine and any products containing active ingredients
  • Discuss your treatment history and any prior reactions to laser or light therapies
  • Assess your skin tone using the Fitzpatrick scale, which informs the appropriate settings for laser treatments
  • Review any medications you are currently taking, as some medications increase photosensitivity or are contraindicated with laser treatments
  • Outline a realistic treatment timeline and number of sessions based on your goal

Preparing for a Laser Treatment

  • Avoid sun exposure, tanning beds, and self-tanner for at least 4 weeks before treatment
  • Avoid retinoids, AHAs, and BHAs for at least 5–7 days before your appointment
  • For laser hair removal: shave the area the day before — do not wax or thread in the weeks prior, as the hair root must be intact
  • Arrive with clean skin, free of makeup, lotion, and fragrance in the treatment area
  • Let us know if you have had any recent sun exposure or skin irritation before your appointment

Aftercare Overview

Aftercare varies by treatment. In general, laser-treated skin requires protection from UV exposure and gentle handling in the days following a session. Avoid active skincare ingredients (retinoids, acids) for a minimum of 5 days post-treatment, and apply a broad-spectrum SPF 30+ daily throughout the full course of treatment. Full aftercare instructions specific to each treatment are on our Post Care page.

How Many Sessions Will I Need?

The number of sessions required depends on the treatment and the concern being addressed.

Laser hair removal typically requires 6–8 sessions spaced 4–8 weeks apart. Hair grows in cycles, and the laser is only effective on follicles in the active growth phase. Multiple sessions ensure that all follicles are treated during their active phase.

BBL photofacial and skin rejuvenation are generally recommended as a series of 3–5 treatments spaced 3–4 weeks apart for optimal results. Annual maintenance treatments are then typical.

Carbon laser peel can produce visible results after a single session and is often done as a series of 4–6 treatments for more pronounced improvement. It can also be done as a standalone session for a special event.

Pigmentation and vascular treatments vary by concern — discrete spots or vessels often clear in 1–3 sessions. We will give you an honest assessment at your consultation.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is laser hair removal permanent?

Laser hair removal provides long-term, significant reduction in hair growth. Most clients achieve 80–90% reduction after a full series of treatments, with minimal regrowth over time. Complete and permanent removal of every follicle is not guaranteed for every individual, but results are typically long-lasting. Hormonal changes can occasionally stimulate new follicle activity, which may require a maintenance session.

Does laser hair removal hurt?

Most clients describe the sensation as a brief snap or heat pulse with each laser pulse. Discomfort varies by treatment area — areas with thinner skin or denser hair tend to be more sensitive. A cooling tip or numbing cream can be used in more sensitive areas. The sensation is typically well-tolerated and brief.

Can I get a BBL photofacial if I have darker skin?

BBL photofacial is generally safest for lighter Fitzpatrick skin types (I–III). For medium to darker skin tones (Fitzpatrick IV–VI), the risk of post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation is higher with broadband light. We will assess your skin tone at the consultation and recommend the most appropriate treatment. In some cases, alternative modalities are better suited.

Is there downtime after laser treatments?

Downtime depends on the treatment. Laser hair removal and carbon laser peel have minimal to no downtime. BBL photofacial may cause mild redness for a few hours post-treatment, and pigmented spots may temporarily darken before flaking off over the following 1–2 weeks. We will walk you through specific aftercare and expected recovery at your appointment.

Can I combine laser treatments with facials or peels?

In many cases, yes — but timing and sequencing matter. Certain combinations are complementary; others require adequate spacing to avoid over-treating the skin barrier. We will map out the safest and most effective treatment order at your consultation based on your full treatment plan.
