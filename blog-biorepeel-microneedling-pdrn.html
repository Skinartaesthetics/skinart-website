BioRePeel + SkinPen Microneedling + PDRN: What This Combination Treatment Does

Anti-Aging October 2026 By Dina Telesh

If you have been researching ways to smooth texture, soften fine lines, and brighten a dull complexion, you have probably seen BioRePeel, SkinPen microneedling, and PDRN mentioned together. Each one is well known on its own. Combined in a single visit, they are designed to work as a team — and it helps to understand what each step is doing before you book.

What is BioRePeel and microneedling combined?

This combination pairs a surface treatment with a deeper one. BioRePeel is a gentle, professional exfoliating peel that helps refine the surface of the skin and brighten tone. SkinPen is an FDA-cleared microneedling device that creates tiny, controlled micro-channels in the skin to encourage its natural renewal process. Used together, they address texture, tone, and fine lines from two directions in the same appointment.

If you would like a refresher on the basics first, our guide to what microneedling treats and what to expect covers the SkinPen side in detail.

Where PDRN fits in

PDRN (polydeoxyribonucleotide) is a skin-conditioning ingredient used in professional skincare for its hydrating, recovery-supporting qualities. Because microneedling temporarily opens micro-channels in the skin, it is a natural moment to support the skin with calming, nourishing ingredients. That is the role PDRN plays in this treatment: it is there to help the skin feel comfortable and cared for while it renews.

What each step does for your skin

BioRePeel. The peel loosens and lifts dull, built-up surface cells, which can help the skin look smoother and more even. It is often chosen as a gentler peel option, which is why it pairs well with other treatments.

SkinPen microneedling. The micro-channels signal the skin to begin its natural renewal process, which over a series of sessions can help improve the look of fine lines, enlarged pores, and uneven texture.

PDRN. Applied as part of the treatment, it supports hydration and comfort during the healing window.

Who this treatment may be a good fit for

This combination is often a good conversation to have if you are noticing a few things at once: skin that looks dull or rough, early fine lines, enlarged pores, or uneven tone. Because it works on both the surface and the deeper renewal process, it suits clients who want more than a standard facial but are not looking for something aggressive.

It is not for everyone. Active breakouts, infections, certain skin conditions, and pregnancy are reasons to wait, and if you have a medical concern about your skin, it is always wise to check with your dermatologist first. That is exactly why we begin with a consultation.

What to expect at your visit

Your esthetician will start with a skin assessment and a conversation about your goals, then cleanse the skin and apply the peel. SkinPen is performed next, followed by soothing, hydrating products including PDRN. Afterward, most clients notice redness similar to a mild sunburn, plus tightness or light flaking over the next few days. Sun protection and gentle products are essential during that time, and we send you home with clear aftercare instructions. Our aftercare guide walks through what to do and avoid.

How many sessions do you need?

Skin renewal takes time, and the best results typically come from a series of treatments spaced several weeks apart rather than a single visit. Your plan depends on your skin, your goals, and how you heal, so we build it together rather than offering a one-size schedule.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What is BioRePeel and microneedling combined?

    It pairs two complementary treatments in one visit: BioRePeel, a gentle exfoliating peel that works on the surface of the skin, and SkinPen microneedling, which creates controlled micro-channels to encourage the skin's natural renewal. Together they address texture, tone, and fine lines from two directions.

  • What is PDRN and why is it added?

    PDRN (polydeoxyribonucleotide) is a skin-conditioning ingredient used in professional skincare to support hydration and the skin's recovery. At SkinArt it is paired with SkinPen so the skin is supported during healing. Your esthetician can explain exactly what is used for your visit.

  • Is there downtime after BioRePeel with microneedling?

    Most clients expect some redness, similar to a mild sunburn, along with tightness or light flaking over the following days. Everyone heals differently, and following your aftercare instructions closely makes a real difference.

  • How many sessions will I need?

    It depends on your goals and how your skin responds. Many clients plan a series of treatments spaced several weeks apart rather than a single visit. A consultation is the best way to build a realistic plan.

  • Who should not have this treatment?

    Microneedling and peels are not appropriate for everyone, including people with active acne breakouts or infections in the treatment area, certain skin conditions, or those who are pregnant. If you have a medical skin concern, please check with your dermatologist first.
